Contest Rules

Last updated: 1 November 2026

These rules apply to every ClipUp round, together with the Terms of Service. Each round also shows its own theme and dates in the app.

How a round works

  1. Open: the round's theme is published. Anyone eligible can enter one video during the entry period and replace it until entries close.
  2. Review: a moderator checks every entry. Entries that break the rules are rejected with a reason; while entries are open, you can upload a new video.
  3. Voting: approved entries are shown to voters.
  4. Results: when voting closes, the results are published automatically in the app.

A round needs at least three approved entries. Otherwise it is cancelled and its videos are deleted.

Entering

  • One entry per person per round.
  • Videos must be vertical, between 5 and 25 seconds, made with AI tools and on the round's theme.
  • You must confirm the declarations shown when you enter (AI-made, you hold the rights, no real person without consent, follows the content rules).

Voting

  • Anyone eligible can vote, including people who entered the round, but nobody can vote for their own video.
  • You must watch a video before you can rank it.
  • Each voter picks their 1st, 2nd and 3rd choice. A 1st place gets 3 points, 2nd gets 2 points and 3rd gets 1 point.
  • Votes from new accounts, suspicious devices or patterns that suggest rigging may be checked and removed.

Results

Entries are ranked by points. Entries with the same points share the place ("joint 2nd", for example). Results are final unless we find that rules were broken, in which case we may disqualify entries and update the results.

The prize

The winner of a round may choose the theme of a future round. They submit a title and a short description within the time shown in the app, and the theme must follow the content rules. A moderator checks it before it is used. If the winner does not choose in time, or the theme is refused, we choose a theme instead. There are no other prizes.

Disqualification

We may disqualify an entry, remove votes or ban accounts for breaking these rules or the Terms, including vote manipulation, using someone else's video or posting banned content.
