Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data ClipUp collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and your rights.

Who we are

ClipUp is run by Ahmed Mahdi, an individual based in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for your personal data. Contact: clipupa@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and confirms you own it; we do not get your Google password.
  • Profile: display name, date of birth (to check you are 18 or over) and country.
  • Your entries: the videos you upload, their titles, descriptions and the AI tools you list, plus moderation decisions about them.
  • Voting: the votes you cast and how much of each video you watched (votes only count after you watch).
  • Fraud prevention: a device identifier created by the app, a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address and the times of your actions. We use these to stop fake accounts and vote rigging.
  • Safety: reports you make, accounts you block and reports made about your entries.
  • Notifications: a push notification token for your phone, and your notification choices.
  • Crash reports: technical details when the app crashes, such as the phone model and the error. These do not include your videos.
  • Legal records: which version of these documents you accepted and when.

Why we use it (and our legal basis under UK GDPR)

  • To run your account and the contests (performing our contract with you): signing you in, showing your entries, counting votes, publishing results and letting winners choose themes.
  • To keep ClipUp fair and safe (our legitimate interests): checking age, moderating videos and themes, detecting fake accounts and vote rigging, and handling reports.
  • To send notifications you asked for (your consent, which you can withdraw in Profile → Notifications or in your phone's settings).
  • To meet legal obligations, for example responding to lawful requests from authorities.

We do not sell your data and we do not show adverts.

What other people can see

Your display name, your approved entries and your placings are public inside the app. Your email address, date of birth and votes are never shown to other users.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run ClipUp. They only process data on our instructions:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and server functions (data stored in the European Union).
  • Mux — video upload, storage and streaming.
  • Google — Google Sign-In, Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications) and Gmail (sending sign-in codes and emails).
  • Hive — automated scanning of video frames to help human moderators spot banned content. It never approves or rejects videos on its own.
  • Sentry — crash reports.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK, such as in the United States. Where they do, we rely on the UK's adequacy regulations or the standard contractual clauses approved for UK data transfers.

How long we keep it

  • Videos: entries that are not approved, or are in a cancelled round, are deleted. Videos from finished rounds are kept for 12 months; the top three of each round stay in the winners' hall of fame.
  • Security and audit logs: 24 months.
  • Your account: until you delete it.

Deleting your account

In the app go to Profile → Delete account. We then remove your name, email address, date of birth, country, notifications, devices, blocks and watch history, and you can no longer sign in. Entries in rounds that are still running are withdrawn. Votes you already cast stay in past results but are no longer linked to your details. See the account deletion page if you can't open the app.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data, or object to or restrict how we use it. You can download your data in the app (Profile → Privacy → Download my data) or email us. We reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk.

Age

ClipUp is only for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and ask you to accept the new version in the app.
