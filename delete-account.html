Delete your ClipUp account

ClipUp is run by Ahmed Mahdi. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open ClipUp and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  3. Confirm.

Without the app

Email clipupa@gmail.com from the email address you use for ClipUp, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your email address, display name, date of birth and country.
  • Your notifications, push tokens, devices, blocks and watch history.
  • Entries in rounds that are still running are withdrawn and their videos deleted.

What is kept

  • Votes you already cast stay in past results but are no longer linked to you.
  • Entries in finished rounds stay without your name until their videos are deleted (after 12 months; the top three of each round stay in the hall of fame).
  • Security and audit logs are kept for up to 24 months to prevent fraud, then deleted.

See the Privacy Policy for details.
